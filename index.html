How it works

  1. 1

    Roll

    Two dice. Their total is the number to make.

  2. 2

    Match

    Pick open tiles that add up to the roll. Match it exactly and they shut.

  3. 3

    Shut the lot

    When no tiles can make the roll, the game ends. Shut every tile and the box is yours.

Three ways to play

  • BeginnerOne row, tiles 1 to 9. The classic pub game.
  • MediumNine rows. Play a tile and it takes the same number above and below it, for a bonus.
  • NinjaNine rows against the clock. 30 seconds a turn.

The Daily

One board a day. Same for everyone.

The same tiles and the same dice for every player, new at midnight. Your first try is the one that counts. Share the result, or pass the phone and play a friend on the same board.
