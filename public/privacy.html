Privacy Policy

What Shut The Cube stores

Shut The Cube stores game progress, high scores, averages, win rates, and display preferences in your browser's local storage. This information stays on your device and is used only to make the game work between visits.

What Shut The Cube does not collect

Shut The Cube does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, payment details, or other personal information. It does not use advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking cookies.

How to remove local game data

You can remove locally stored game information at any time through your browser's site-data or local-storage settings. Removing that data resets your game records and preferences.

Third-party services

The game is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process limited technical information, such as IP address and request logs, to deliver the site under its own policies.

Changes to this policy

If the game begins using analytics, advertising, accounts, or other services that process personal information, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect.
