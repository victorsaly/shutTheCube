Shut The Cube

How to play

Shut the Box, the pub dice game, plus a nine-row board where a matching column collapses in one move.

The aim

Shut every tile. Each turn you roll the dice, then pick open tiles that add up to exactly what you rolled. When no tiles can make the roll, the game ends. Your score is the total of the tiles you shut.

A turn

  1. Roll the dice. Their total is the number to make.
  2. Pick open tiles that add up to it. The sum under the board shows what you have picked and how much is still to go.
  3. Match it exactly and those tiles shut. Roll again.

In Beginner, once the 7, 8 and 9 are shut you may roll one die. When 6 or less is left on any board, the second die is dropped for you.

Three modes

  • BeginnerOne row, tiles 1 to 9. The classic pub game.
  • MediumNine rows. Matching columns collapse together for a bonus.
  • NinjaNine rows against the clock. 30 seconds a turn.

Columns that collapse

On the nine-row boards, playing a tile also takes the same number directly above and below it, as far as the run continues. The extra tiles score as a bonus; only the tile you picked counts toward the roll. A small badge on a tile shows how many tiles it will take.

Special tiles and events

Medium and Ninja boards have a few special tiles. A Wild tile, marked with a star, counts as any number you still need. A Locked tile, marked with a diamond, plays only on its own, when it equals the whole roll. About one turn in six brings an event: a lucky third die, a reshuffle of the rows, or a tile turning wild. Every shut tile still scores its face value.

The Daily

The Today tab deals one board a day for each mode, the same tiles and dice for everyone, new at midnight. Your first try is the one that counts. Sign in if you want your name on the day's leaderboard; everything else works without it.

Pass and play

Two players take turns on one device with the identical board. The first score stays hidden until both have played. The higher total wins; if both shut the box, fewer rolls wins.

Keys and gestures

On a phone, tap the tiles and shake to roll. On a keyboard, Space rolls, the arrow keys move between tiles and Enter picks one, U undoes a pick and H shows a hint.
