How to Play Shut The Cube

A free dice-and-tiles browser game with the familiar Shut the Box challenge and a nine-layer cube twist.

Objective

Roll the dice and close every tile. On each turn, choose one or more open tiles whose values add up exactly to your roll. When you cannot make a valid combination, the game ends. Clear the board to Shut the Box.

How a Turn Works

  1. Roll the dice.
  2. Select open tiles that add up to the roll.
  3. Commit the matching combination and roll again.

In Classic mode, you may roll one die once all remaining tiles are 6 or lower.

Game Modes

  • BeginnerClassic one-row Shut the Box.
  • MediumNine rows. Matching tile columns collapse together for bonus points.
  • NinjaThe nine-row cube game with 30 seconds per turn.

The Cube Bonus

In the nine-row modes, playing a tile can also claim matching faces immediately above and below it. The claimed run scores as a bonus, but only the tiles you selected count toward the current dice roll. A badge previews how many tiles a move will claim.

Special Tiles and Events

Medium and Ninja games can include special tiles and occasional between-turn events. A Wild tile can count as whatever value is still needed. A Locked tile can only be played alone and must match the entire roll. Lucky third-die, reshuffle, and wild-tile events can change a turn, but every closed tile always scores its face value.

Controls

Press Space to roll. Use Tab to reach the board, arrow keys to move between tiles, Enter to select a tile, U to undo a selection, and H for a hint. On supported phones, shake to roll.
