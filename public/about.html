Shut The Cube

A pub dice game, nine rows deep

What it is

Shut The Cube is a free browser game inspired by the classic Shut the Box dice game. It keeps the simple challenge of shutting tiles that match each roll, adds a nine-row board where matching columns collapse for a bonus, and deals one shared board a day.

Made to play, not collect data

There is no advertising and no tracking, and you do not need an account. Your games, best scores, averages and win rate stay on your device. Signing in is optional and does one thing: it puts a name beside your score on the daily leaderboard. The privacy page has the detail.

Open source

Shut The Cube is created by Victor Saly. The source code, development notes, and issue tracker are available on GitHub.
