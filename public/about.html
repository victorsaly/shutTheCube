About Shut The Cube

A familiar dice game with a new dimension

Shut The Cube is a free browser game inspired by the classic Shut the Box dice game. It keeps the simple challenge of closing tiles that match each roll and adds a nine-layer cube mode where matching columns collapse for bonus points.

Made to play, not collect data

There is no account, advertising, or player tracking. Your games, best scores, averages, and win rate stay in your browser's local storage and do not leave your device.

Open source

Shut The Cube is created by Victor Saly. The source code, development notes, and issue tracker are available on GitHub.
